NextbotCreator uses open-source Rust crates listed in Cargo.lock. Their license metadata and source links are available through crates.io.

Portable distributions include FFmpeg 8.1.2 Essentials Build by Gyan Doshi. That Windows build is licensed under GPLv3 and its corresponding FFmpeg source is available at:
https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-8.1.2.tar.xz

The distribution also includes the FFmpeg build's license as tools/ffmpeg-license.txt.

Binary build and checksum:
https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/packages/ffmpeg-8.1.2-essentials_build.zip
https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/packages/ffmpeg-8.1.2-essentials_build.zip.sha256

Portable distributions include yt-dlp 2026.08.19 (with its bundled Python interpreter and EJS solver):
https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/releases/tag/2026.08.19
yt-dlp source is under the Unlicense; the combined Windows executable includes GPLv3+ components.
The matching license and third-party notices are included as tools/yt-dlp-license.txt and tools/yt-dlp-third-party-licenses.txt.
The app's explicit downloader update action installs the corresponding notices alongside each verified update.
Source: https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/tree/2026.08.19

Portable distributions include Deno 2.9.6, licensed under MIT, to run the YouTube challenge solver:
https://github.com/denoland/deno/releases/tag/v2.9.6
Source: https://github.com/denoland/deno/tree/v2.9.6
Its license is included as tools/deno-license.txt. Notices for its V8 engine and TypeScript compiler are included as tools/deno-third-party-licenses.txt.
